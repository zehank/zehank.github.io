About关于我

In grade one I filled pages with interlocking trap mechanisms — a mouse, a piece of cheese, and a chain of contraptions that fired one after another. The drawings kept getting more complicated. That habit never really went away; it just changed material.

A few years later I saw a model airplane flying over a square on the way home from my mother's office, and I would not stop asking for one. What arrived was a box of parts. My dad helped me assemble my first pneumatic model plane; it crashed after a few flights.

In high school I went back to the same idea, but much harder. As a self-initiated project-based-learning (PBL) project I designed and built an FPV quadcopter: a custom flight-controller PCB, the flight firmware, matching motors, props and a carbon frame, a video transmitter, GPS, and full PID tuning — all soldered into one airframe. No teacher at my school knew the subject, so the project was mine; my dad could only help with soldering. I burned my hands, destroyed boards, mounted props backwards, and once blew a capacitor the instant I connected the battery. I finished it anyway — then kept adding endurance batteries, LED strips for night flight, an ND filter on the camera, head-tracking and a gimbal.

After that I founded two more PBL projects — a KNSB solid-fuel rocket test rig and an AI vision obstacle-avoidance car — and started my school's tech club so other students could build things too.

To me, electrical engineering is the most powerful workbench humans have. Arrange a handful of diodes and transistors into logic, and you can build almost anything. My long-term goal is human–machine integration: interfaces that let people share senses and knowledge directly. That is why I am studying EE.

小学一年级，我在纸上画满了一整套联动陷阱：一只老鼠、一块奶酪，和一环扣一环的机关。越画越复杂。这个习惯其实一直没消失，只是换了材料。

后来有一次和妈妈下班的路上，我看到广场上有架航模在飞，就吵着要买。到手的却只是一盒零件。爸爸帮我装好了人生第一台气动航模——飞了几次就摔坏了。

高中我又回到了同一件事上，但难度完全不同。作为我自己开设的项目化学习（PBL）项目，我设计并制作了一台 FPV 穿越机：自己设计的飞控 PCB、飞控代码、电机/桨叶/碳纤维机身的合理选配、图传系统、GPS，以及完整的 PID 调试，最后全部焊进同一台机身。学校里没有老师懂这个方向，所以项目完全是我自己的，爸爸能帮的只有焊接。我烫伤过手，烧坏过不少电路板，把桨叶装反过，还有一次因为虚焊，接上电池的瞬间电容就爆了。但最后我把它做成了——之后又不断加装：更高密度的电池、夜间飞行的 LED 灯带、图传上的 ND 滤镜、头追装置和云台。

再之后我又开了两个 PBL 项目——KNSB 固体燃料火箭测试台，和一台 AI 视觉避障小车——并创办了学校的科技社团，让其他同学也能动手做东西。

在我看来，电子工程是人类最强大的工作台。把一把二极管和晶体管排成逻辑，你几乎能做任何东西。我的长期目标是"人机结合"：让人可以直接共享感官与知识。这也是我选择电气工程的原因。

Custom FPV Quadcopter自制 FPV 穿越机

Founder & lead — Ruian High School PBL项目开创者与负责人 —— 瑞安中学 PBL

Targets: roughly 300 km/h top speed, ~10 km control range, live video to FPV goggles, and GPS. Built with no teacher guidance and no published build guide to follow.

  • Designed the flight-controller PCB myself and soldered the FC, ESCs, motors, GPS, video transmitter and battery harness into one airframe.
  • Matched motors, propellers and a carbon frame as a system; tuned PID loops; studied RF protocols and antenna placement to reach the range target.
  • Failures I worked through: burns and destroyed boards from poor soldering, propellers mounted backwards, and a cold solder joint that burst a capacitor the moment the battery was connected.
  • Kept extending it — higher-density batteries for endurance, LED strips for night flight, an ND filter on the camera, head tracking and a camera gimbal for 360° view following.
  • Learned the underlying theory from English datasheets and niche forums because so little was documented.

目标：时速约 300 km/h、遥控距离约 10 km、画面实时回传到 FPV 眼镜、带 GPS 定位。全程没有任何老师指导，也没有现成的教程可以照搬。

  • 自己设计飞控 PCB，并把飞控、电调、电机、GPS、图传和电池接口全部焊接进同一台机身。

PBL2025

KNSB Solid-Fuel Rocket & Test RigKNSB 固体燃料火箭与测试台

Founder & lead — self-funded项目开创者与负责人 —— 自费

I wrote the project plan, the milestone reports and the final report, sourced every material myself, and led a team that joined to learn rather than to deliver.

  • Synthesized KNSB propellant from potassium nitrate and sorbitol, and assembled the lab kit: thermostatic hotplate, precision thermometer, precision balance, glassware, crucibles and a grain sealing setup.
  • Built the thrust test stand — an aluminium frame carrying a precision load cell wired to an Arduino sampling at 80 Hz and logging to an SD card.
  • Designed and 3D-printed the rocket body and fins, made the parachute, built the remote ignition system, and ran static and flight tests.
  • Hardest problem: powerful oxidizers are nationally controlled and cannot be bought. We bought weaker precursors and used electrolysis to prepare what we could not source — which capped the achievable specific impulse, and that ceiling is what really limited the rocket.
  • Ran everything with remote ignition, respirators and written procedure; the charred casing in these photos is what a static test does to hardware.

计划书、阶段性报告和结题报告都由我撰写，材料全部自费采购；组员更像是带着进来体验项目的参与者，而不是来交付任务的。

  • 用硝酸钾和山梨糖醇制备 KNSB 燃料，并配齐实验器材：恒温加热台、精密温度计、精密天平、烧杯量筒试管坩埚与药柱密封装置。
  • 搭建推力测试台：铝合金支架固定精密压力传感器，接 Arduino 以 80 Hz 采样并把数据写入 SD 卡。
  • 3D 建模并打印箭体与尾翼、制作降落伞、搭远程引爆装置，完成静态试车与飞行试验。
  • 最大的困难：强氧化性原料在国内属于管控物资，市面上买不到。我们只能买氧化性较弱的原料，再通过电解池分解制备买不到的部分——这也直接压低了可达到的比冲，是限制火箭水平的关键。
  • 全程采用远程点火、防毒面具和书面操作流程；照片里烧蚀发黑的壳体，就是一次静态试车对硬件做的事。

AI Vision Obstacle-Avoidance CarAI 视觉避障小车

Team lead — embedded, vision and control组长 —— 负责嵌入式、视觉与控制

An embedded car that drives under remote control, sees with a camera, avoids obstacles and runs an object detector I trained.

  • Platform: Raspberry Pi, DC motors with ESCs, camera, battery and chassis. I was team lead and did the core work myself, assigning simple assembly steps to the others.
  • Owned the full stack: wiring (motors → ESC → Pi, camera → Pi), flashing the Raspberry Pi OS image, the Python control code, and the obstacle-avoidance logic.
  • Collected 100 photographs and trained a detector to recognise a cup and a person.
  • Problem: recognition was unreliable when we trained on one person from one angle. Fix: we re-shot the set with four team members photographed from four directions (front, back, left, right). Accuracy improved substantially.
  • Final demo: the car completed the test track with avoidance, followed by a project presentation.

一台可以远程遥控行驶、用摄像头"看"、能自动避障，并跑着我亲自训练的目标检测模型的嵌入式小车。

  • 硬件构成：树莓派、带电调的直流电机、摄像头、电源和塑料车身。我担任组长并操刀核心部分，简单的组装会分配给其他组员。
  • 整条链路都由我负责：电路连接（电机→电调→树莓派，摄像头→树莓派）、树莓派系统烧录、Python 控制代码，以及避障逻辑。

  • 问题：只用同一个人、同一个角度训练时识别率不高。解决：改用 4 名组员从前后左右 4 个方向拍摄的 100 张照片，识别率明显提升。
  • 最终成果：小车跑完赛道并完成避障，随后进行项目汇报展示。

For my school2024

School Conduct-Score Website学校操行分记录网站

My school tracked student conduct and merit points on paper. I moved it onto a website so it was easier to operate and the records were openly visible.

  • Score entry and public records, plus automatically generated per-student summaries with line charts showing whether performance was improving or slipping.
  • Stack: HTML, JavaScript and CSS on the front end; MySQL for data; deployed behind Nginx/Apache over HTTP/HTTPS.
  • Debugging story: front end and back end would not stay in sync. The cause was my TCP implementation only completing a two-step handshake, which also broke the HTTP layer above it. Implementing a proper three-way handshake made transfers accurate, non-duplicating and stable.
  • Motivation was simple: leave the school something useful.

学校的操行品德分原本靠人工统计。我把它搬到网站上，操作更方便，记录信息公开透明。

  • 分数录入与公开查询，并自动生成个人概况：折线图 + 表现评语（有进步或欠佳）。
  • 技术栈：前端 HTML / JavaScript / CSS；数据库 MySQL；部署在 Nginx/Apache 上，走 HTTP/HTTPS。
  • 踩坑：前后端数据无法同步。原因是我写的 TCP 只做了两次握手，连带把上层的 HTTP 传输也搞坏了。补全三次握手后，数据传输变得准确、不重复且稳定。
  • 动机很简单：给学校留下点自己的贡献。

SanGuoSha — multiplayer card game in C++三国杀 —— C++ 多人联机桌游

I rebuilt the SanGuoSha board game in C++ with multiplayer networking, to train my programming and logic.

  • Roughly 32 KB of game logic plus a networked multiplayer layer.
  • Hardest part: the game's judgement rules. Once heroes and card types multiply, each phase runs different checks and operations, so the logic has to be written rigorously in advance — sloppy code silently skips a phase or judges it wrongly, and in the worst case deadlocks during a match.
  • Debugging means reasoning about many special cases to find latent bugs, which took most of the time.

为了锻炼编程能力和逻辑思维，我用 C++ 把三国杀这张桌游重做了一遍，并做了多人联机。

  • 约 32 KB 的游戏逻辑，外加一层联机组网。
  • 最难的部分是判定规则。当人物和牌的类型多起来后，每个阶段都要做不同的判定和操作，逻辑必须提前写严谨——写得草率，轻则跳过某个阶段或判断错误，重则在对局中直接卡死。
  • 调试要穷举大量特殊情况去找潜在 bug，这部分花掉了最多时间。

Where this is going我想去的地方

A long-horizon research direction, not a product plan. It is the reason I chose EE. 这是一个远期研究方向，不是产品计划。它是我选择电气工程的原因。

Human–machine integration人机结合

My goal is a coin-sized module that integrates compute and radios and connects directly to the body — to sensory nerves, and to the body's own energy supply. Not a device you carry, but one you are.

I am at the very beginning of this. First-year EE is the first real step, and the honest version of this page is that most of it is unsolved.

我的目标是一个硬币大小的模块：把算力和无线通信集成进去，直接与人体连接——连到各处感官神经，也连到身体自身的供能系统。不是随身携带的设备，而是与你共生的一部分。

我现在只是刚起步。大一电气工程是第一步，而这一页最诚实的说法是：其中大部分问题都还没有解。

  1. Power from the body从身体取电

    Replace the battery with harvested energy: biofuel cells converting glucose or lactate from body fluid, thermoelectric generators using the body-to-ambient temperature difference, and piezoelectric nanogenerators collecting energy from muscle movement — several weak sources combined into a stable one. 用能量收集取代电池：生物燃料电池把体液中的葡萄糖或乳酸转化为电能，热电材料利用人体与环境的温差发电，压电纳米发电机从肌肉运动中收集微弱电能——多个弱源叠加成一个稳定的源。

  2. Display, without a screen不要屏幕的显示

    Intercept the visual signal travelling from the eye to the brain, combine it with a rendered image, then re-encode the result back into a neural signal — so the eye itself becomes the camera and the overlay lands in your actual field of view. The hard part is not rendering, it is the electrical-to-neural translation, and doing it fast enough. 在视觉信号从眼睛传入大脑的路径上做拦截，把它与生成的画面结合，再重新编码回神经信号——让眼睛本身成为摄像头，叠加的画面落在你真实的视野里。难点不在渲染，而在电信号与神经信号之间的转译，以及足够快的处理速度。

  3. A path that starts with medicine一条从医疗开始的路

    The credible way in is clinical, not consumer: start with a mechanical eye for blind patients — a CMOS sensor that converts light to electrical signal and drives the visual cortex — and use it to derive and validate the translation between electrical and neural signals. Everything else follows from getting that right. 真正可行的入口是临床，而不是消费电子：先为盲人做一只机械眼——用 CMOS 感光传感器把光转为电信号，再送入视觉中枢——借此推导并验证电信号与神经信号的转译关系。把这一步做对，后面的东西才谈得上。

  4. Interaction, sensing and sharing交互、感知与共享

    Intent recognition instead of typing; reading and writing data at the speed of thought; silent conversation over cellular links, whether or not the person on the other end has the implant. And eventually live streams that carry sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch together — presence in the real sense. 用意图识别代替打字；以思维的速度读写数据；通过蜂窝网络进行"不开口"的通话——对方有没有装同样的设备都能接通。最终，直播不再只共享视觉，而是视听嗅味触同时共享——真正意义上的身临其境。

This direction raises hard ethical questions — consent, access, and what happens to people who opt out — and I do not think they can be deferred until the technology works. I intend to keep them in view from the start. 这个方向伴随着棘手的伦理问题——知情同意、可及性，以及选择不接入的人会怎样——我不认为这些可以等技术成熟了再讨论。我希望从一开始就把它放在视野里。
